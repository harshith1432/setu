Super Admin Emergency Actuator Overrides

High-priority immediate commands that bypass automatic software rules during heatwaves, power anomalies, or equipment faults.

Master Command

Emergency Exhaust Ventilation

Instantly engage high-velocity ventilation fans at 100% duty cycle to evacuate ammonia buildup and trapped heat.

Emergency Fogger Boost (Rapid Cool)

Force cooling fogger misting pumps to rapidly drive down internal shed temperatures during severe ambient heat spikes.

Emergency Master Equipment Shutdown

Safely turn OFF all heaters, cooling foggers, fans, and lamps instantly. Use during sensor faults, electrical shorts, or repairs.

Emergency Fresh Air & CO₂ Evacuation

Force high-velocity exhaust fans to 100% capacity to flush out carbon dioxide or ammonia buildup when air quality deteriorates.

Emergency Photoperiod Darkness Lock

Instantly switch OFF all shed illumination (0% PWM) to enforce rest and soothe stressed or agitated silkworms.

Inspection Illumination Override

Engage photoperiod lamps at uniform 40% PWM for cluster officers and veterinarians inspecting trays without thermal glare.
