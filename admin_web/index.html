Targeted Applications

Engineered Solutions for Every Sericulture Stage

Silkworm larvae (*Bombyx mori*) are poikilothermic organisms requiring stage-specific environmental controls to thrive and spin premium cocoons.

Instars 1 to 2

Chawki Rearing Climate Stabilization

Challenge Chawki worms require 85%–90% RH and 27°C–28°C. Droplet condensation rots mulberry bed leaves, causing disease.
Solution Ultrasonic piezoceramic fogging in regulated pulses without wet droplet precipitation, combined with draft-free gentle convection.
Outcome Vigorous, uniform moulting with zero bed rot and maximum larval survival into late age.
Instars 3 to 5

Late-Age Heat & Gas Extraction

Challenge Rapid biomass accumulation generates heavy metabolic heat, moisture, CO₂, and ammonia build-up inside the rearing hall.
Solution Targeted thermal pull-down to 23°C–25°C, high-volume exhaust air exchange, and continuous Winsen MQ-135 air monitoring.
Outcome Elimination of larval suffocation, prevention of grasserie outbreaks, and optimal cocoon shell weight.
Fail-Safe Engineering

Automated Safety Interlocks & Redundancy

Challenge Erratic grid voltage, sensor faults, or conflicting actuator triggers can overheat or flood a rearing shed.
Solution Physical dual-relay interlock prevents mutual activation of heating and cooling circuits. Dual SHT31 probes validate readings before firing actuators.
Outcome Zero accidental thermal collisions, zero relay sticking, and immediate WhatsApp alert if sensor drift occurs.
Enterprise Operations

Cooperative & Regional Cluster Operations

Challenge Sericulture officers and cluster managers lack unified visibility across hundreds of dispersed farm sheds.
Solution Centralized Super Admin console with multi-tenant access, trilingual voice alerts (Kannada, Telugu, English), and technician dispatch tracking.
Outcome High accountability, streamlined field support, and unified quality grading across farming clusters.
Operating Architecture

From Shed Floor to Closed-Loop Automation

A reliable 6-stage telemetry and control pipeline that operates 24/7 with zero dependence on continuous external connectivity.

01

Deploy Hardware & Probes

Mount the industrial Setu IoT Gateway and position Sensirion SHT31 dual-probe thermal/humidity sensors at worm-bed level.

02

Continuous Edge Sampling

The dual-core ESP32-S3 edge node samples temperature, humidity, air quality (MQ-135), and light lux (BH1750) sub-second intervals.

03

Encrypted Ingestion Stream

Telemetry is packaged with cryptographic mTLS device credentials and streamed via MQTT 5 / REST to PostgreSQL time-series tables.

04

Evaluate Agronomic Setpoints

The rule engine compares current microclimate data against CSRTI-certified ranges configured for the shed's active instar stage.

05

Execute Closed-Loop Actuation

Actuator commands toggle zero-crossing PWM dimmers, exhaust fans, cold mist foggers, or PTC heaters through interlocked safety relays.

06

Farmer Alerts & AI Explanations

If manual intervention is needed, WhatsApp voice notes and notifications explain the exact condition in Kannada, Telugu, or English.

Zero-Single-Point-of-Failure Architecture

If broadband drops, the edge node continues autonomous PID microclimate regulation using local flash setpoints, then buffers and backfills telemetry when connection is restored.

SeriCare is an industrial IoT microclimate automation and farm intelligence platform engineered specifically for silkworm rearing sheds (Bombyx mori):

The platform actively monitors and manages four critical physical parameters essential for larval growth and cocoon quality:

  • Temperature Control: ±0.2°C precision via dual-redundant Sensirion SHT31 industrial probes, preventing heat shock and larval stagnation.
  • Relative Humidity: ±1.5% RH precision hygrometry regulating ultrasonic cold-mist foggers and evaporative cooling pads.
  • Bed Air Quality & Gases: Real-time detection of carbon dioxide (CO₂) and toxic ammonia fumes using pre-calibrated Winsen MQ-135 sensors.
  • Circadian Illuminance: 0–65,535 Lux sensing via BH1750 digital sensors, maintaining optimal photoperiods for synchronized larval feeding.
Automated Actuation: Industrial optocoupled controllers regulate evaporative coolers, heating elements, ultrasonic foggers, exhaust ventilation fans, and zero-crossing PWM lighting.

The platform supports open agricultural and industrial IoT hardware standards:

  • Supported Sensor Probes: Industrial RS-485 Modbus RTU and digital I²C sensors including Sensirion SHT3x/SHT4x, Winsen MQ-135, BH1750 lux meters, and Dallas DS18B20 temperature probes.
  • Actuator Controllers: 4-channel solid-state contactors (rated up to 16A @ 230V AC), zero-crossing AC PWM light dimmers (up to 1,000W), and reversible ventilation blowers.
  • Hardware Cryptography: Dual-core ESP32-S3 edge controllers secured with hardware cryptographic elements and mTLS X.509 device certificates.

Turnkey on-site deployment is executed by certified SeriCare field technicians in a structured 5-step process (typically 3 to 5 hours):

  • 1. Shed Thermal Audit: Assessment of rearing shed dimensions, thermal insulation, airflow dead zones, and existing electrical distribution.

  • 5. Multi-Point Calibration: Performing reference-instrument calibration, pairing the farmer mobile application, and verifying automated actuation.

Privacy Policy & Data Governance

1. Agricultural Telemetry Ownership

All microclimate telemetry collected from rearing shed nodes (temperature, humidity, CO₂, photoperiod) remains the intellectual and operational property of the registered farmer and operating organization. SeriCare uses this data solely to execute automated actuation, trigger safety alerts, and improve agronomic models.

2. Telemetry Security & Encryption

Data transmission between shed gateways and cloud servers is secured via mutual TLS (mTLS) with hardware X.509 certificates. Databases employ role-based access control (RBAC) and row-level multi-tenant isolation in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.

3. Contact & Inquiry Privacy

Contact details submitted through our website are used exclusively by our engineering and sales representatives to respond to inquiries and coordinate hardware installations. We never sell or share contact details with third-party advertising networks.

Terms of Service & Warranty

1. Hardware Warranty Coverage

All SeriCare industrial gateway nodes, sensor probes, and relay controllers carry a standard 12-month manufacturer replacement warranty against manufacturing defects and component failures under standard agricultural operating conditions.

2. Hardware Safety Interlocks & Disclaimers

SeriCare hardware incorporates hardware-level mutual exclusion relays. Users must adhere to certified electrical connection standards and use the physical manual override switches in extreme emergency conditions. SeriCare is not liable for damages caused by unauthorized electrical tampering.

3. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

Commercial support subscribers receive guaranteed 4-hour remote response and 24-hour on-site technician dispatch during active silkworm rearing crop cycles across primary Karnataka sericulture clusters.
